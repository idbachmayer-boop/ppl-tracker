SCHEMA and MIGRATIONS are the project's own schema-change rule (CLAUDE.md, Conventions: Schema). They are not among the five derived consumers SLEEP-04 names, and REG-11 keeps MIGRATIONS hand-written by design. The bump is also what protects the new data: `remoteTooNew()` makes an older cached build refuse to push over a schema-18 blob. Without it, an old build would carry sleep wholesale instead of unioning it.

Deliberately left alone: `snapSummary()`, the cosmetic count line on Settings → Version history. It is not one of the five consumers SLEEP-04 names, so leaving it untouched is intentional, not a missed edit (01-RESEARCH.md, Open Question 2). `exportData()`, the sync push, `restoreSnapshot()`, `wipe()` and `importData()` work on the whole DB and pick sleep up with no change.
