<objective>
Ship the Content-Security-Policy as one revertable commit (D-06): the `<meta>` policy built up from
`default-src 'none'` (D-01, D-02, D-07, D-10), its property tests, the deploy-time `--check`, and the
CLAUDE.md note (CSP-07). Then prove it: every guard red against its own regression, zero violations
in real Chrome on desktop and Android user agents, and a clean revert rehearsal.

Purpose: escaping (`esc()`) is the primary XSS control and has known holes. A hash-only
`script-src` means a missed escape can no longer run script, and `connect-src` means data cannot be
posted to a foreign host. The policy must never be the thing that silently kills sync, which is
why every Firebase and weather host is inventoried by hand and every guard fails `npm test`.

Output: one CSP commit (CSP_SHA, the revert target) and the proof record in the SUMMARY. Nothing is
pushed. 07-05 ships it after the CSP-05 human pass.
</objective>
