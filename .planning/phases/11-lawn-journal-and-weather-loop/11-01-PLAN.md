<objective>
Stop the weather fetch from looping when the phone is offline, and make the test harness able to see
a loop if one ever comes back.

- The harness `fetch` settles and counts every call (LAWN-07, D-16).
- A failed fetch backs off 5 → 15 → 60 minutes on Today and on Care → Lawn. A manual tap, or the phone
  coming back online on a weather screen, retries at once (LAWN-06, D-14).
- Today fetches all year (D-12).
- While failing, Lawn keeps the last forecast with an offline note, or says it couldn't load weather.
  An old day is never shown as today (D-15, RESEARCH Pattern 8).

Purpose: today a stale cache on Care → Lawn makes 41 fetches in 40 event-loop turns (RESEARCH Pattern 3,
measured), and the inert harness stub hid it (04-REVIEW WR-01, WR-05). This plan goes first, so every
later plan in the phase is tested against a fetch that settles. Plan 11-02 uses the `opts.fetch`
injection point it adds.

Output:
- `test/harness.js`: the new stub, injection, counters, window-listener recording.
- `index.html`: the backoff, the online retry, Today's all-year fetch, the S5 and S6 states.
- `test/app.test.js`: a new LAWN section and the fetch-ledger backstop.
</objective>
