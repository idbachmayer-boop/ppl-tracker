<objective>
Save each finished day's rainfall, with its exact amount, in a new synced `rainLog` collection, and get
it to Ian's other device without letting a stale device look newest (LAWN-02).

- One `COLLECTIONS` entry, placed last, plus SCHEMA 20 and migration 20, by the CLAUDE.md recipe.
- `recordRain()` writes one row per finished day from the weather response, now 14 past days (D-02).
- The quiet push: `touch()` + `saveLocal()` + `schedulePush()`, never `save()` (D-01).

Purpose: this is the phase's one data change. The rows are written by the device, read by both devices,
and kept in every backup from then on. 11-04 reads them for the rain marks. The quiet push is how a
derived row reaches the cloud without reopening the 2026-07-25 hole that the weather cache once opened.
