<objective>
Hold off watering on cool days and on days whose own forecast is wet, decide watering in one place, and
mark the next watering day in the outlook (LAWN-01, LAWN-04).

- `waterVerdict(o)` takes over lawnStatus's inline watering chain and adds the cool-day hold (D-09) and the
  wet-forecast hold (D-08).
- `nextWaterDay()` finds the one day that gets the DROPLET (D-07).
- The Mowing outlook becomes the Lawn outlook: UMBRELLA for "wet", the DROPLET for "water", pair cells, a
  water line and a footnote built from the constants (D-06).

Purpose: Today's card, the heads-up, the "next …" line and the strip all read the same verdict, so they
can never disagree (D-08). It is November, so the phone cannot show any of this until April. The suite
carries it, under the frozen August clock.

- `index.html`: two constants, two new functions, the lawnStatus mapping, mowMark and mowOutlookHTML.
- `test/harness.js`: four names.
- `test/app.test.js`: LAWN-01 and LAWN-04 checks, wording rows, S1 checks.
</objective>
