pulldown — skipped: shoulder") and on the next card's Last time line ("Skipped Oct 13: shoulder"), which
says why there is no Add weight. Re-opening the workout to edit it keeps the skip.

D-24) and keeps it through the edit path (D-25). Every skip text after the name comes from 10-01's
skipTailHTML, so the card, the history line and the inline rows (10-03) cannot drift.

Output:

  and editSession carrying the marker.
