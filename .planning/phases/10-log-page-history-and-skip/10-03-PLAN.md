workout, with deloads, skips and PRs marked. One tap more opens its full chart in Progress → Strength,
and Back to workout puts him exactly where he was: same scroll spot, same panel open, collapsed cards
still collapsed, every set untouched.

text) and in the router change that every tab arrival goes through.
