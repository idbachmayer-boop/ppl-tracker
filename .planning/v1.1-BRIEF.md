# v1.1 brief: the progression fixes, lawn, sleep and strength ideas

Agreed with Ian on 2026-10-09 in a grilling session. Source: his 14 in-app ideas (Aug 10 to Oct 9) plus two
follow-ups from the v1.0 audit. Every rule below is a decision Ian approved, not a suggestion.

## Out of scope

- The v1.0 CSP-test hardening (07-REVIEW WR-01..03) runs as a separate quick task.
- Kettlebells. Ian doesn't use them, so there is no kettlebell equipment type and no list of bells.

## Phases, in order

### 1. Progression correctness (checked with a real workout on the phone)

- **Add weight** only when every planned set for the slot was done (none skipped, none missing) and each
  one reached the top of the rep range at the working weight. A skipped set blocks the increase.
  - Today `addWeightInfo` filters out skipped and blank sets, so 2 sets at the top plus 1 skipped still
    says "add weight". That is the bug.
- **Progress**: compare each session with the previous one. It counts as progress if total reps across
  all sets went up by at least 1, or if the weight went up.
- **Stall**: 3 sessions in a row with no progress, measured against the previous session, never the
  all-time best. Track by exercise identity (`exKey`), not the exact name, so renames and merges keep
  their history.
  - Today `isStalledSlot` uses the best single-set e1RM against the all-time max before the last 3
    sessions, so extra reps on sets 2 and 3 never count. That causes the false DB lateral raise warning
    and the repeating stalls.
- **Bodyweight exercises** stall too, with progress meaning +1 total rep. At the top of the range on all
  sets, show "add weight (belt) or harder variation", with no number.

### 2. Equipment and weight steps (real workout)

- **Equipment type** on each exercise: barbell, EZ bar, dumbbell, machine, cable or bodyweight. Defaulted by
  name and editable per exercise in Settings → Exercises. It is stored on the synced exercise-registry row.
- **Steps**: barbell, EZ bar, machine and cable go up 5 lb; dumbbells go up 2.5 lb. Deloads and warm-ups
  round to the exercise's own step.
  - This fixes the deload that does nothing: 15 lb × 0.9 currently rounds back to 15.
  - Today the step is a flat 5 lb, and `round5` / `translateLoad` are applied everywhere.
- **Plate math** follows the equipment type. **Preacher curl** is an EZ bar exercise with a 25 lb bar, so it
  gets plate math.

### 3. Log page (real workout)

- **History mid-workout**: tapping the "Last time" line on an exercise card opens that exercise's last 5
  sessions inline, with a link to the full chart in Progress → Strength. The workout is never left.
- **Skip exercise**: a button on each card, with one optional reason. It skips all of that exercise's sets,
  can be undone, and counts as skipped, so it blocks adding weight next time.

### 4. Lawn and journal (automated checks plus a quick look on the phone)

- **Too cool**: hold off watering when today's forecast high is below 60°F.
- **Rain history**: once a day is over, save that day's rainfall in its own small synced record, separate
  from `lawnLog`. Separate because `mergeDateMap` takes the whole inner object from the newer side, and
  rain written into `lawnLog` could overwrite another device's watered/mowed marks. 0.1 inches or more
  shows 🌧 in lawn history, and the exact amount is always kept.
  - Today only 3 past days of weather exist, in the `DB.wx` cache.
  - This is a new collection, so it follows CLAUDE.md "Adding a new tracked thing": the entry goes last,
    plus a SCHEMA bump and a migration.
- **Watering in the outlook**: the 7-day outlook shows 💧 on the day watering is next due. It skips any day
  where forecast rain meets the season's `rainSkip` (0.3 to 0.5 inches) or the chance of rain is 60% or
  higher.
- **Journal**: Week in Review shows 🚜 Mowed, 💧 Watered and 🌧 rain on each day.
- **Weather retry loop** (v1.0 audit, 04-REVIEW WR-01): a failed weather fetch must not loop
  fetch → render endlessly. Back off. Fix the test harness's never-settling `fetch` (WR-05) too, so the loop
  can't hide.

### 5. Sleep (automated checks plus a quick look on the phone)

- **Sleep card on Today**: shows in the morning until last night's sleep is logged, then disappears for the
  day. It is dated the day you woke up, takes hours and quality, and the note is optional. Reuse the
  morning weigh-in card's pattern.
- **Erase keeps the workout in progress** (v1.0 audit): `wipe()` sets `DB=blank()` without
  `keepLocalDraft`. Route it through the helper, or document it in CLAUDE.md as the one exception.

### 6. Strength index and poor-sleep intensity (automated checks plus a quick look on the phone)

- **Strength index**: 100 = where you started. For every exercise trained in the last 4 weeks, divide its
  best e1RM from those weeks by its best from its own first 4 weeks logged, then average across exercises.
  - Bodyweight exercises use total reps in place of e1RM.
  - A second line shows the index adjusted for bodyweight: index × (bodyweight at start ÷ bodyweight now).
  - It appears at the top of Progress → Strength with a weekly chart, plus one small number on Today's
    week card, e.g. "Strength 112, +3 this month".
- **Poor sleep makes the workout lighter**:
  - Poor sleep means quality 2 or lower, or under 6 hours, in last night's sleep entry.
  - When the workout starts, ask: "You slept poorly. Go 10% lighter today?" with one tap. It is never
    automatic.
  - Lighter keeps the same sets and reps, with suggested weights cut 10% and rounded to the exercise's
    step, and always at least one step lighter.
  - A lighter session is marked like a deload, so it never counts as a stall or as a reason to add weight.
  - If no sleep is logged, nothing changes, and the Log page shows a small "Log last night's sleep" link.
    Logging a poor night there brings up the offer.

## Rules that carry over

All of CLAUDE.md applies:
- Union-merge sync, soft deletes and explicit `false`.
- Migrations that `touch()` and persist.
- Derived data uses `saveLocal`.
- The draft never leaves the device.
- `esc()` on everything a user can control.
- `data-action` delegation.
- `npm run csp:hash` after any change to the inline script.
- New fetch hosts need a CSP change.
